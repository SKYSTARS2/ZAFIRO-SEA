About Zafiro Sea Tourism Company

Zafiro Sea Tourism Company is a premier travel and tourism company based in Hurghada, Egypt, dedicated to creating unforgettable experiences across the breathtaking Red Sea. Our passion is to connect travelers with the natural beauty, vibrant marine life, and rich culture that make Hurghada one of the world's most popular holiday destinations[cite: 1].

We specialize in organizing high-quality sea excursions, snorkeling and diving adventures, private yacht charters, island trips, desert safaris, cultural excursions, airport transfers, and customized holiday packages. Every experience is carefully designed to combine comfort, safety, and exceptional service.

At Zafiro Sea Tourism Company, customer satisfaction is at the heart of everything we do. Our experienced team works closely with every guest to provide personalized travel experiences that exceed expectations. Whether you are seeking adventure, relaxation, or a family vacation, we ensure every journey becomes a lasting memory.

Our Mission

To deliver safe, reliable, and memorable travel experiences while showcasing the beauty of the Red Sea and the hospitality of Egypt.

Our Vision

To become one of the leading tourism companies in Hurghada, recognized internationally for excellence, innovation, and outstanding customer service.
